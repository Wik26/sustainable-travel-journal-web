GoTravel is a website designed to encourage eco-conscious mindsets among travellers.

It aims to promote sustainable travel habits and improve overall environmental awareness by discouraging harmful tourism activities.

The platform includes a travel journal to document trips, and a carbon footprint calculator that estimates emissions.
